Where it helps and where it hurts

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • The lowest price per gigabyte of memory among the providers we have priced, at every size from 2 GB up
  • Memory, processors and disk are chosen separately, so a disk-hungry tool does not force a bigger plan
  • Billed by the minute on hourly plans, which suits short trials
  • The Starter (Type A) servers priced here are described by VPSServer as meant for always-on tasks rather than high-speed performance; its Standard and Enterprise types cost more
  • Its own terms place it in the same corporate group as Kamatera, so the two are not independent suppliers

Configurable servers billed monthly, or by the minute on hourly plans. 1 to 5 TB of traffic a month is included depending on location, and extra traffic is $0.01 per GB. Prices exclude VAT.
