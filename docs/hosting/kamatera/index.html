Where it helps and where it hurts

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • Its 1 GB plan is the cheapest 1 GB server among the providers we have priced
  • The fixed plans run on dedicated CPU threads, according to the pricing page
  • Billed by the minute, which suits short trials
  • Every fixed plan includes only 20 GB of disk, so most multi-container tools need paid extra storage
  • Above 1 GB of memory, the fixed plans cost more than DigitalOcean for the same RAM

Fixed monthly plans, billed by the minute. The pricing page lists extra traffic at $0.01 per GB and extra storage at $0.05 per GB per month; neither is included in the figures on this site.
