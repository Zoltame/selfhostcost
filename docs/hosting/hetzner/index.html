Where it helps and where it hurts

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • The cheapest memory of any provider we have priced: 8 GB for EUR 8.99 a month on the Cost-Optimized line
  • 20 TB of traffic included on every plan
  • German company with European data centres, which settles most data-residency questions
  • Hetzner sells the Cost-Optimized line for test and development environments, with variable CPU performance, so the cheapest rows here are not a like-for-like swap for a dedicated-thread server
  • Prices exclude VAT and are quoted in euros, so a non-European buyer carries the exchange-rate risk
  • No block storage price is recorded here yet, so storage-heavy tools do not pick Hetzner

Hourly billing capped at the monthly price shown. Prices are quoted in euros excluding VAT and converted once at the dated rate on the methodology page. Every plan includes 20 TB of traffic.
