Hetzner Cloud is based in Germany. We have not been able to read its prices programmatically, so no figure on this site is based on Hetzner Cloud. The specifications below are confirmed; the prices are not.

Where it helps and where it hurts

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • Best price per GB of RAM among mainstream hosts

  • EU, US and Singapore regions only
  • EUR billing adds currency risk for USD budgets
  • Popular plans go out of stock
