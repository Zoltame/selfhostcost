Which one to pick

FreeScout 4,532 stars

Laravel and MySQL. A shared mailbox rather than a full support suite, which is exactly what most small teams are overpaying Zendesk for.

At 10 people it needs 1.4 GB of memory and 13 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Help Scout?

    There are 1 on this page, and every one of them is free of licence cost: they are published under AGPL-3.0. Running one is not free, though. The cheapest of them, FreeScout, costs $45.54 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Help Scout cost?

    Help Scout Standard costs $300 a month at 10 people: USD 30.00 per seat per month; free for up to 5 users. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-14.

    What does it cost to self-host FreeScout?

    $45.54 a month at 10 people. That is $6.70 for the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud, plus backups, plus 0.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before FreeScout is cheaper than Help Scout?

    under a month, counting $150 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $9,011.

    Which Help Scout alternative is the easiest to run?

    FreeScout, rated easy: about 2 hours to set up and 0.5 hours a month afterwards.
