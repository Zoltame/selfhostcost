Which one to pick

Uptime Kuma 91,286 stars

One container with SQLite. Cost scales with the number of monitors and their check interval, not with the number of people reading the dashboard.

At 10 people it needs 0.6 GB of memory and 5.1 GB of disk, which fits the Basic at Kamatera for $4.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Better Stack Uptime?

    There are 1 on this page, and every one of them is free of licence cost: they are published under MIT. Running one is not free, though. The cheapest of them, Uptime Kuma, costs $42.30 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Better Stack Uptime cost?

    Better Stack Uptime Responder costs $34.00 a month at 10 people: USD 34.00 per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-14.

    What does it cost to self-host Uptime Kuma?

    $42.30 a month at 10 people. That is $4.00 for the Basic at Kamatera, plus backups, plus 0.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    never, counting $150 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $449.
