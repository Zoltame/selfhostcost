Open-source alternatives to Sentry, priced

2 open-source tools that can replace Sentry, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Sentry on the Team tier costs $26.00 a month at that size.

The cheapest way to replace Sentry here is GlitchTip, at $139 a month against $26.00, with your own time already counted.

Which one to pick

GlitchTip

Sentry-SDK compatible, so existing client instrumentation keeps working unchanged. Far lighter than self-hosted Sentry, which needs Kafka and ClickHouse. Source lives on GitLab rather than GitHub, so the star count shown elsewhere on this site is not available for it.

At 10 people it needs 2.6 GB of memory and 20.5 GB of disk, which fits the Starter 4 GB at VPSServer for $22.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

SigNoz 32,081 stars

OpenTelemetry-native traces, metrics and logs over ClickHouse. Replaces a Datadog bill rather than just a Sentry bill, and the retention window drives the disk cost.

At 10 people it needs 5.1 GB of memory and 51 GB of disk, which fits the Starter 8 GB at VPSServer for $39.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 16 hours of work, then 3 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Sentry?

    There are 2 on this page. 1 of them are open source, published under MIT, with no licence to buy. SigNoz ships its source under its own licence rather than a standard open-source one, so check its terms before commercial use. None of them is free to run: the cheapest, GlitchTip, costs $139 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Sentry cost?

    Sentry Team costs $26.00 a month at 10 people: USD 26.00 per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-10-09.

    What does it cost to self-host GlitchTip?

    $139 a month at 10 people. That is $22.00 for the Starter 4 GB at VPSServer, plus backups, plus 1.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before GlitchTip is cheaper than Sentry?

    never, counting $450 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $4,514.

    Which Sentry alternative is the easiest to run?

    GlitchTip, rated medium: about 6 hours to set up and 1.5 hours a month afterwards.
