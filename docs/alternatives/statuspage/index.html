Self-hosted alternatives to Atlassian Statuspage, priced

2 open-source tools that can replace Atlassian Statuspage, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Atlassian Statuspage on the Startup tier costs $99.00 a month at that size.

Which one to pick

Uptime Kuma 91,286 stars

One container with SQLite. Cost scales with the number of monitors and their check interval, not with the number of people reading the dashboard.

At 10 people it needs 0.6 GB of memory and 5.1 GB of disk, which fits the Basic at Kamatera for $4.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Gatus 12,056 stars

Configuration as YAML in version control, which suits teams that want their monitoring reviewed like code. The smallest footprint on this entire site.

At 10 people it needs 0.3 GB of memory and 2.1 GB of disk, which fits the Basic 512 MiB at DigitalOcean for $4.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.
