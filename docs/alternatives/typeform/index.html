Open-source alternatives to Typeform, priced

3 open-source tools that can replace Typeform, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Typeform on the Plus tier costs $56.00 a month at that size.

The cheapest way to replace Typeform here is OpnForm, at $45.54 a month against $56.00, with your own time already counted.

Which one to pick

OpnForm 3,696 stars

Laravel and Vue over PostgreSQL. The most direct Typeform substitute for plain form building.

At 10 people it needs 1.3 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Full Typeform versus OpnForm breakdown · OpnForm at every team size

LimeSurvey 3,717 stars

Two decades of survey logic, including quotas and complex branching that the newer tools do not attempt. The interface shows its age.

At 10 people it needs 1.3 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Full Typeform versus LimeSurvey breakdown · LimeSurvey at every team size

Formbricks 12,928 stars

Next.js with PostgreSQL. Aimed at in-product surveys as much as standalone forms, which is where Typeform's per-response pricing bites hardest.

At 10 people it needs 2.6 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Typeform?

    There are 3 on this page. 0 of them are open source, published under no standard open-source licence, with no licence to buy. OpnForm, LimeSurvey, Formbricks ships its source under its own licence rather than a standard open-source one, so check its terms before commercial use. None of them is free to run: the cheapest, OpnForm, costs $45.54 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Typeform cost?

    Typeform Plus costs $56.00 a month at 10 people: USD 56.00 per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-12.

    What does it cost to self-host OpnForm?

    $45.54 a month at 10 people. That is $6.70 for the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud, plus backups, plus 0.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before OpnForm is cheaper than Typeform?

    14 months, counting $150 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $227.

    Which Typeform alternative is the easiest to run?

    OpnForm, rated easy: about 2 hours to set up and 0.5 hours afterwards.
