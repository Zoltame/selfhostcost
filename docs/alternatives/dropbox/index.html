File sync and storage

Open-source alternatives to Dropbox Business, priced

3 open-source tools that can replace Dropbox Business, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Dropbox Business on the Standard tier costs $134 a month at that size.

The cheapest way to replace Dropbox Business here is Nextcloud, at $150 a month against $134, with your own time already counted.

Which one to pick

Nextcloud 36,777 stars

Disk per user is the line that decides this comparison, not RAM. At 15 GB a head, storage overtakes compute well before 50 users.

At 10 people it needs 2.8 GB of memory and 170 GB of disk, which fits the Starter 4 GB plus a 90 GB volume at VPSServer for $31.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Seafile 15,236 stars

Block-level sync makes it noticeably faster than Nextcloud on large files, at the cost of a much smaller app ecosystem.

At 10 people it needs 2.7 GB of memory and 170 GB of disk, which fits the Starter 4 GB plus a 90 GB volume at VPSServer for $31.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Full Dropbox Business versus Seafile breakdown · Seafile at every team size

Immich 113,881 stars

Server, machine-learning container, PostgreSQL with pgvector, and Redis. The ML container is what sets the 4 GB floor, and photo storage dominates the total cost.

At 10 people it needs 5.2 GB of memory and 620 GB of disk, which fits the Starter 8 GB plus a 470 GB volume at VPSServer for $86.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 16 hours of work, then 3 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Dropbox Business?

    There are 3 on this page. 2 of them are open source, published under AGPL-3.0, with no licence to buy. Seafile ships its source under its own licence rather than a standard open-source one, so check its terms before commercial use. None of them is free to run: the cheapest, Nextcloud, costs $150 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Dropbox Business cost?

    Dropbox Business Standard costs $134 a month at 10 people: EUR 12.00 per seat per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-10-09.

    What does it cost to self-host Nextcloud?

    $150 a month at 10 people. That is $31.00 for the Starter 4 GB plus a 90 GB volume at VPSServer, plus backups, plus 1.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before Nextcloud is cheaper than Dropbox Business?

    Never, counting $450 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $1,004.

    Which Dropbox Business alternative is the easiest to run?

    Nextcloud, rated medium: about 6 hours to set up and 1.5 hours afterwards.
