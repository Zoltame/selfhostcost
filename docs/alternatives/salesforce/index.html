Open-source alternatives to Salesforce Sales Cloud, priced

2 open-source tools that can replace Salesforce Sales Cloud, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Salesforce Sales Cloud on the Pro Suite tier costs $1,000 a month at that size.

The cheapest way to replace Salesforce Sales Cloud here is EspoCRM, at $45.54 a month against $1,000, with your own time already counted.

Which one to pick

EspoCRM 3,343 stars

PHP and MySQL, and by far the cheapest way to run a real CRM. Less polished than Twenty but stable and easy to back up.

At 10 people it needs 1.4 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Full Salesforce Sales Cloud versus EspoCRM breakdown · EspoCRM at every team size

Twenty 56,624 stars

Server, worker, PostgreSQL and Redis. The most credible modern-feeling open CRM, and still moving fast enough that you should pin versions.

At 10 people it needs 5.2 GB of memory and 21 GB of disk, which fits the CX33 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $10.06 a month. Setting it up is roughly 16 hours of work, then 3 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Salesforce Sales Cloud?

    There are 2 on this page. 1 of them are open source, published under AGPL-3.0, with no licence to buy. Twenty ships its source under its own licence rather than a standard open-source one, so check its terms before commercial use. None of them is free to run: the cheapest, EspoCRM, costs $45.54 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Salesforce Sales Cloud cost?

    Salesforce Sales Cloud Pro Suite costs $1,000 a month at 10 people: USD 100.00 per seat per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-10-09.

    What does it cost to self-host EspoCRM?

    $45.54 a month at 10 people. That is $6.70 for the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud, plus backups, plus 0.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before EspoCRM is cheaper than Salesforce Sales Cloud?

    under a month, counting $150 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $34,211.

    Which Salesforce Sales Cloud alternative is the easiest to run?

    EspoCRM, rated easy: about 2 hours to set up and 0.5 hours a month afterwards.
