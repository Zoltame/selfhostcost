Identity and SSO

Open-source alternatives to Auth0, priced

3 open-source tools that can replace Auth0, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Auth0 on the Essentials (B2B) tier costs $150 a month at that size.

The cheapest way to replace Auth0 here is Keycloak, at $121 a month against $150, with your own time already counted.

Start here

Auth0's free tier covers up to 25,000 monthly active users. Below that ceiling, and without needing the paid B2B features, self-hosting an identity provider saves nothing at all. The paid comparison below only applies once you need what the Essentials tier adds.

Which one to pick

Keycloak 36,732 stars

JVM service with PostgreSQL. Memory is dominated by the heap rather than by user count, so cost per user falls steeply as the directory grows. This is the CNCF-backed incumbent.

At 10 people it needs 2.5 GB of memory and 10.1 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

authentik 25,446 stars

Server plus worker, PostgreSQL and Redis. Friendlier than Keycloak for proxy-style protection of apps that have no native SSO support.

At 10 people it needs 2.5 GB of memory and 10.1 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Full Auth0 versus authentik breakdown · authentik at every team size

Zitadel 15,005 stars

Go binary over PostgreSQL, with an event-sourced audit log. Lighter than the JVM option and closer to Auth0's developer ergonomics.

At 10 people it needs 2.5 GB of memory and 10.1 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Auth0?

    There are 3 on this page. 2 of them are open source, published under AGPL-3.0, Apache-2.0, with no licence to buy. authentik ships its source under its own licence rather than a standard open-source one, so check its terms before commercial use. None of them is free to run: the cheapest, Keycloak, costs $121 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Auth0 cost?

    Auth0 Essentials (B2B) costs $150 a month at 10 people: USD 150.00 per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-12.

    What does it cost to self-host Keycloak?

    $121 a month at 10 people. That is $6.70 for the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud, plus backups, plus 1.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before Keycloak is cheaper than Auth0?

    15 months, counting $450 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $611.

    Which Auth0 alternative is the easiest to run?

    Keycloak, rated medium: about 6 hours to set up and 1.5 hours a month afterwards.
