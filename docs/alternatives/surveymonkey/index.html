Self-hosted alternatives to SurveyMonkey, priced

2 open-source tools that can replace SurveyMonkey, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. SurveyMonkey on the Team Advantage tier costs $348 a month at that size.

Which one to pick

LimeSurvey 3,717 stars

Two decades of survey logic, including quotas and complex branching that the newer tools do not attempt. The interface shows its age.

At 10 people it needs 1.3 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk, which fits the Basic 2 GiB at DigitalOcean for $12.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Formbricks 12,928 stars

Next.js with PostgreSQL. Aimed at in-product surveys as much as standalone forms, which is where Typeform's per-response pricing bites hardest.

At 10 people it needs 2.6 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk, which fits the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.
