Which one to pick

Windmill 17,901 stars

Rust server with PostgreSQL and one or more workers. Faster per job than the Node-based options, which matters if the workflow count is high.

At 10 people it needs 2.6 GB of memory and 16 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Retool?

    There are 1 on this page. 0 of them are open source, published under no standard open-source licence, with no licence to buy. Windmill ships its source under its own licence rather than a standard open-source one, so check its terms before commercial use. None of them is free to run: the cheapest, Windmill, costs $121 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Retool cost?

    Retool Team costs $55.96 a month at 10 people: EUR 5.00 per seat per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-14.

    What does it cost to self-host Windmill?

    $121 a month at 10 people. That is $6.70 for the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud, plus backups, plus 1.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before Windmill is cheaper than Retool?

    Never, counting $450 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $2,775.

    Which Retool alternative is the easiest to run?

    Windmill, rated medium: about 6 hours to set up and 1.5 hours afterwards.
