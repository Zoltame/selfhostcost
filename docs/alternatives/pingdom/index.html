Open-source alternatives to Pingdom, priced

2 open-source tools that can replace Pingdom, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Pingdom on the Synthetic Monitoring tier costs $16.04 a month at that size.

The cheapest way to replace Pingdom here is Uptime Kuma, at $42.30 a month against $16.04, with your own time already counted.

Which one to pick

Uptime Kuma 91,286 stars

One container with SQLite. Cost scales with the number of monitors and their check interval, not with the number of people reading the dashboard.

At 10 people it needs 0.6 GB of memory and 5.1 GB of disk, which fits the Basic at Kamatera for $4.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Full Pingdom versus Uptime Kuma breakdown · Uptime Kuma at every team size

Gatus 12,056 stars

Configuration as YAML in version control, which suits teams that want their monitoring reviewed like code. The smallest footprint on this entire site.

At 10 people it needs 0.3 GB of memory and 2.1 GB of disk, which fits the Basic 512 MiB at DigitalOcean for $4.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Pingdom?

    There are 2 on this page, and every one of them is free of licence cost: they are published under Apache-2.0, MIT. Running one is not free, though. The cheapest of them, Uptime Kuma, costs $42.30 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Pingdom cost?

    Pingdom Synthetic Monitoring costs $16.04 a month at 10 people: EUR 14.33 per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-14.

    What does it cost to self-host Uptime Kuma?

    $42.30 a month at 10 people. That is $4.00 for the Basic at Kamatera, plus backups, plus 0.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before Uptime Kuma is cheaper than Pingdom?

    never, counting $150 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $1,095.

    Which Pingdom alternative is the easiest to run?

    Uptime Kuma, rated easy: about 2 hours to set up and 0.5 hours a month afterwards.
