Self-hosted alternatives to Tableau, priced

2 open-source tools that can replace Tableau, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Tableau on the Tableau Standard tier costs $150 a month at that size.

Monthly cost at 10 people. Self-hosted totals include the server, backups and 75-dollar-an-hour maintenance time.
Tool Server only With your time vs Tableau Operational weight Licence
Metabase $28.80 $141 -$137 medium source-available
Apache Superset $57.60 $283 -$5,974 hard Apache-2.0

The final column is the 36-month difference: positive means self-hosting is cheaper.

Which one to pick

Metabase 49,210 stars

JVM application with an application database of its own. Concurrent query load, not seat count, is what forces an upgrade here.

At 10 people it needs 2.7 GB of memory and 10.5 GB of disk, which fits the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Apache Superset 74,726 stars

Web process, Celery workers, beat scheduler, PostgreSQL and Redis. More capable than Metabase and considerably more to operate.

At 10 people it needs 5.2 GB of memory and 20.5 GB of disk, which fits the Basic 8 GiB at DigitalOcean for $48.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 16 hours of work, then 3 hours a month to keep it healthy.
