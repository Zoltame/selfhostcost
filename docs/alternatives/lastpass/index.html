Open-source alternatives to LastPass, priced

2 open-source tools that can replace LastPass, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. LastPass on the Teams / Business tier costs $49.47 a month at that size.

The cheapest way to replace LastPass here is Vaultwarden, at $42.30 a month against $49.47, with your own time already counted.

Which one to pick

Vaultwarden 67,195 stars

A Rust reimplementation of the Bitwarden server. It runs comfortably in a fraction of a gigabyte because vault data is small and encrypted client-side.

At 10 people it needs 0.3 GB of memory and 2.2 GB of disk, which fits the Basic 512 MiB at DigitalOcean for $4.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 2 hours of work, then 0.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Full LastPass versus Vaultwarden breakdown · Vaultwarden at every team size

Passbolt 6,115 stars

PHP application with MariaDB. GPG key handling makes onboarding slower than Vaultwarden but the audit trail is stronger.

At 10 people it needs 1.3 GB of memory and 5.2 GB of disk, which fits the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to LastPass?

    There are 2 on this page, and every one of them is free of licence cost: they are published under AGPL-3.0. Running one is not free, though. The cheapest of them, Vaultwarden, costs $42.30 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does LastPass cost?

    LastPass Teams / Business costs $49.47 a month at 10 people: EUR 4.42 per seat up to 50 users, EUR 6.50 per seat above that, per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-14.

    What does it cost to self-host Vaultwarden?

    $42.30 a month at 10 people. That is $4.00 for the Basic 512 MiB at DigitalOcean, plus backups, plus 0.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before Vaultwarden is cheaper than LastPass?

    21 months, counting $150 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $108.

    Which LastPass alternative is the easiest to run?

    Vaultwarden, rated easy: about 2 hours to set up and 0.5 hours a month afterwards.
