Which one to pick

Chatwoot 36,724 stars

Rails web process plus a Sidekiq worker, PostgreSQL and Redis. The worker is not optional; skipping it silently breaks outbound email and webhooks.

At 10 people it needs 5.4 GB of memory and 28 GB of disk, which fits the CX33 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $10.06 a month. Setting it up is roughly 16 hours of work, then 3 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Intercom?

    There are 1 on this page. 0 of them are open source, published under no standard open-source licence, with no licence to buy. Chatwoot ships its source under its own licence rather than a standard open-source one, so check its terms before commercial use. None of them is free to run: the cheapest, Chatwoot, costs $237 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Intercom cost?

    Intercom Essential costs $290 a month at 10 people: USD 29.00 per seat per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-14.

    What does it cost to self-host Chatwoot?

    $237 a month at 10 people. That is $10.06 for the CX33 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud, plus backups, plus 3 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before Chatwoot is cheaper than Intercom?

    23 months, counting $1,200 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $705.

    Which Intercom alternative is the easiest to run?

    Chatwoot, rated hard: about 16 hours to set up and 3 hours afterwards.
