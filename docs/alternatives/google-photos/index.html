Open-source alternatives to Google One storage, priced

2 open-source tools that can replace Google One storage, each shown with what it would actually cost to run for a team of 10 people. Google One storage on the Google AI Plus (2 TB) tier costs $11.58 a month at that size.

The cheapest way to replace Google One storage here is PhotoPrism, at $195 a month against $11.58, with your own time already counted.

Start here

The plan compared here is the largest one Google's public plans page listed on 2026-10-09: 2 TB, shareable with up to five people. Above that, this comparison stops being like for like, because the self-hosted figures size storage to whatever the team actually stores while the Google plan stays capped at 2 TB. Google sells larger plans, but their prices were not published on that page.

Which one to pick

PhotoPrism 40,183 stars

Go with MariaDB and on-device classification. Lighter than Immich but its multi-user story is weaker.

At 10 people it needs 2.7 GB of memory and 620 GB of disk, which fits the Pro plus a 600 GB volume at Kamatera for $69.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 6 hours of work, then 1.5 hours a month to keep it healthy.

Immich 113,881 stars

Server, machine-learning container, PostgreSQL with pgvector, and Redis. The ML container is what sets the 4 GB floor, and photo storage dominates the total cost.

At 10 people it needs 5.2 GB of memory and 620 GB of disk, which fits the Starter 8 GB plus a 470 GB volume at VPSServer for $86.00 a month. Setting it up is roughly 16 hours of work, then 3 hours a month to keep it healthy.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a free open-source alternative to Google One storage?

    There are 2 on this page. 1 of them are open source, published under AGPL-3.0, with no licence to buy. PhotoPrism ships its source under its own licence rather than a standard open-source one, so check its terms before commercial use. None of them is free to run: the cheapest, PhotoPrism, costs $195 a month at 10 people once the server, backups and the time to look after it are counted.

    How much does Google One storage cost?

    Google One storage Google AI Plus (2 TB) costs $11.58 a month at 10 people: EUR 9.99 per month. That figure was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-10-09.

    What does it cost to self-host PhotoPrism?

    $195 a month at 10 people. That is $69.00 for the Pro plus a 600 GB volume at Kamatera, plus backups, plus 1.5 hours a month of your own time priced at the published hourly rate.

    How long before PhotoPrism is cheaper than Google One storage?

    never, counting $450 of first-time setup. Over 36 months the difference is $7,064.

    Which Google One storage alternative is the easiest to run?

    PhotoPrism, rated medium: about 6 hours to set up and 1.5 hours a month afterwards.
