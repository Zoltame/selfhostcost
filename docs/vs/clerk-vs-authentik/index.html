Read this before you price anything

Clerk's Hobby plan is free for up to 50,000 monthly retained users. An internal team will almost never exceed that, so for most readers the honest licence cost is zero and the paid comparison below only applies if you need what Pro adds.

What authentik needs to run at 10 people

Server plus worker, PostgreSQL and Redis. Friendlier than Keycloak for proxy-style protection of apps that have no native SSO support.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Clerk has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $4.80 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • authentik is a multi-container stack with a database you have to back up yourself. That is why the maintenance line is 1.5 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. authentik covers the core of what teams use Clerk for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

We are not going to tell you to self-host this

At 10 people, Clerk wins on cost once your time is priced at $75 an hour. Self-hosting authentik here only makes sense for reasons that are not on the invoice: data residency, avoiding a vendor, or wanting to run the thing yourself. Those are real reasons. Saving money is not one of them at this size.

Where to put authentik

2.5 GB of memory and 10.1 GB of disk is what this needs at 10 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month.
