What Nextcloud needs to run at 10 people

Disk per user is the line that decides this comparison, not RAM. At 15 GB a head, storage overtakes compute well before 50 users.

ResourceRequiredHow it is derived

vCPU2 The project's own baseline. For this workload memory runs out before the processor does.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Dropbox Business has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $6.20 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • Nextcloud is a multi-container stack with a database you have to back up yourself. That is why the maintenance line is 1.5 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. Nextcloud covers the core of what teams use Dropbox Business for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

We are not going to tell you to self-host this

At 10 people, Dropbox Business wins on cost once your time is priced at $75 an hour. Self-hosting Nextcloud here only makes sense for reasons that are not on the invoice: data residency, avoiding a vendor, or wanting to run the thing yourself. Those are real reasons. Saving money is not one of them at this size.
