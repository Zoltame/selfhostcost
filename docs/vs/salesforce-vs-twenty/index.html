What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Salesforce Sales Cloud has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $7.80 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • Twenty is operationally heavy: several stateful services and an upgrade path that can lose data if you rush it. That is why the maintenance line is 3 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. Twenty covers the core of what teams use Salesforce Sales Cloud for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.
