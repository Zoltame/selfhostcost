What Zammad needs to run at 10 people

Rails with PostgreSQL, Redis and Elasticsearch. Elasticsearch is what makes this the heaviest helpdesk here, and it is effectively required for usable search.

ResourceRequiredHow it is derived

vCPU2 The project's own baseline. For this workload memory runs out before the processor does.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Zendesk has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $9.60 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • Zammad is operationally heavy: several stateful services and an upgrade path that can lose data if you rush it. That is why the maintenance line is 3 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. Zammad covers the core of what teams use Zendesk for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

Where to put Zammad

5.4 GB of memory and 33 GB of disk is what this needs at 10 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the Basic 8 GiB at DigitalOcean for $48.00 a month.
