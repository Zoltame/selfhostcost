What Activepieces needs to run at 10 people

Application, PostgreSQL and Redis. Isolated sandboxes for code steps are what push the memory floor above n8n's.

ResourceRequiredHow it is derived
Memory2.7 GB 2 GB for the stack at rest, plus 15 MB per active user, plus 25% headroom
Disk11 GB 10 GB base, plus 0.1 GB per user
vCPU2 The project's own baseline. For this workload memory runs out before the processor does.

These base figures are an estimate. Activepieces does not publish a minimum specification we could cite, so the base memory and disk are derived from the services in its own reference Docker Compose file. Treat them as a starting point and watch the first week of metrics.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Make has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $4.80 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • Activepieces is a multi-container stack with a database you have to back up yourself. That is why the maintenance line is 1.5 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. Activepieces covers the core of what teams use Make for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

We are not going to tell you to self-host this

At 10 people, Make wins on cost once your time is priced at $75 an hour. Self-hosting Activepieces here only makes sense for reasons that are not on the invoice: data residency, avoiding a vendor, or wanting to run the thing yourself. Those are real reasons. Saving money is not one of them at this size.

Where to put Activepieces

2.7 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk is what this needs at 10 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month.
