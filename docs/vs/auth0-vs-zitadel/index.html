Auth0 on the Essentials (B2B) tier against Zitadel running on the smallest server that fits it. Every figure below is computed from the sizing model and the dated prices at the foot of this page, and includes the part most comparisons leave out: your own time.

Read this before you price anything

Auth0's free tier covers up to 25,000 monthly active users. Below that ceiling, and without needing the paid B2B features, self-hosting an identity provider saves nothing at all. The paid comparison below only applies once you need what the Essentials tier adds.

What Zitadel needs to run at 10 people

Go binary over PostgreSQL, with an event-sourced audit log. Lighter than the JVM option and closer to Auth0's developer ergonomics.

ResourceRequiredHow it is derived
Memory2.5 GB 2 GB for the stack at rest, plus 2 MB per active user, plus 25% headroom

vCPU2 The project's own baseline. For this workload memory runs out before the processor does.

These base figures are an estimate. Zitadel does not publish a minimum specification we could cite, so the base memory and disk are derived from the services in its own reference Docker Compose file. Treat them as a starting point and watch the first week of metrics.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Auth0 has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $1.34 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • Zitadel is a multi-container stack with a database you have to back up yourself. That is why the maintenance line is 1.5 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. Zitadel covers the core of what teams use Auth0 for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

Where to put Zitadel

2.5 GB of memory and 10.1 GB of disk is what this needs at 10 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month.
