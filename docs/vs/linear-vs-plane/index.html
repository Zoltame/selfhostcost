Linear vs self-hosted Plane: what each actually costs

Linear on the Basic tier against Plane running on the smallest server that fits it. Every figure below is computed from the sizing model and the dated prices at the foot of this page, and includes the part most comparisons leave out: your own time.

At 10 people this one is close enough to go either way. The server costs $57.60 a month against $100 for Linear, but add 3 hours a month of your attention and self-hosting lands at $283 and loses. It only wins if your time is genuinely free.

What Plane needs to run at 10 people

Django API, web and space frontends, PostgreSQL, Redis, RabbitMQ and MinIO. A genuine Jira substitute and priced like one in RAM terms.

ResourceRequiredHow it is derived
Memory5.2 GB 4 GB for the stack at rest, plus 20 MB per active user, plus 25% headroom
Disk27 GB 25 GB base, plus 0.2 GB per user
vCPU2 The project's own baseline. For this workload memory runs out before the processor does.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Linear has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $9.60 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • Plane is operationally heavy: several stateful services and an upgrade path that can lose data if you rush it. That is why the maintenance line is 3 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. Plane covers the core of what teams use Linear for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

We are not going to tell you to self-host this

At 10 people, Linear wins on cost once your time is priced at $75 an hour. Self-hosting Plane here only makes sense for reasons that are not on the invoice: data residency, avoiding a vendor, or wanting to run the thing yourself. Those are real reasons. Saving money is not one of them at this size.

Where to put Plane

5.2 GB of memory and 27 GB of disk is what this needs at 10 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the Basic 8 GiB at DigitalOcean for $48.00 a month.
