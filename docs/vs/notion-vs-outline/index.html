What Outline needs to run at 10 people

Needs PostgreSQL, Redis and S3-compatible object storage for attachments. The object storage requirement is the part people miss when budgeting.

ResourceRequiredHow it is derived

vCPU2 The project's own baseline. For this workload memory runs out before the processor does.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Notion has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $4.80 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • Outline is a multi-container stack with a database you have to back up yourself. That is why the maintenance line is 1.5 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. Outline covers the core of what teams use Notion for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

Where to put Outline

2.6 GB of memory and 18 GB of disk is what this needs at 10 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month.
