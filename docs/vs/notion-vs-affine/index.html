What AFFiNE needs to run at 10 people

The closest match to Notion's block editor and whiteboard combination. Self-hosting is supported but moves faster than the other options here, so pin your image tag.

ResourceRequiredHow it is derived
Memory2.7 GB 2 GB for the stack at rest, plus 15 MB per active user, plus 25% headroom

vCPU2 The project's own baseline. For this workload memory runs out before the processor does.

These base figures are an estimate. AFFiNE does not publish a minimum specification we could cite, so the base memory and disk are derived from the services in its own reference Docker Compose file. Treat them as a starting point and watch the first week of metrics.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Notion has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $1.34 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • AFFiNE is a multi-container stack with a database you have to back up yourself. That is why the maintenance line is 1.5 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. AFFiNE covers the core of what teams use Notion for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

Where to put AFFiNE

2.7 GB of memory and 19 GB of disk is what this needs at 10 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the CX23 (cost-optimized) at Hetzner Cloud for $6.70 a month.
