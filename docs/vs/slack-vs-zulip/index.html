What Zulip needs to run at 10 people

Django with PostgreSQL, Redis, RabbitMQ and memcached. The topic-threaded model is genuinely better for asynchronous work than Slack's channels.

ResourceRequiredHow it is derived

vCPU2 The project's own baseline. For this workload memory runs out before the processor does.

What you give up, stated plainly

  • Uptime is now yours. Slack has an operations team on call. Self-hosted, a failed upgrade at 18:00 on a Friday is your evening.
  • Backups only exist if you test restoring them. The $7.80 line above buys snapshots, not a rehearsed recovery.
  • Zulip is operationally heavy: several stateful services and an upgrade path that can lose data if you rush it. That is why the maintenance line is 3 hours a month rather than zero.
  • Feature parity is never exact. Zulip covers the core of what teams use Slack for; the long tail of integrations and enterprise controls is where the gap shows.

We are not going to tell you to self-host this

At 10 people, Slack wins on cost once your time is priced at $75 an hour. Self-hosting Zulip here only makes sense for reasons that are not on the invoice: data residency, avoiding a vendor, or wanting to run the thing yourself. Those are real reasons. Saving money is not one of them at this size.
