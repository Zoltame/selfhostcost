How 11.1 GB was arrived at

4 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 250 users at 20 MB each adds 4.9 GB, giving 8.9 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 11.1 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Starter 16 GB plus a 14.4 TB volume with 16 GB.

Disk works the same way: 20 GB to begin with, plus 60 GB per user, so 14.7 TB against the 300 GB the plan includes. The shortfall is bought as a 14.4 TB volume at $1,472 a month, which costs less than the next plan up. This tool is storage-hungry per user, so disk rather than memory is what will force your next upgrade.

Server, machine-learning container, PostgreSQL with pgvector, and Redis. The ML container is what sets the 4 GB floor, and photo storage dominates the total cost.
