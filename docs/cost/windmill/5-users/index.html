How 2.6 GB was arrived at

2 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 5 users at 10 MB each adds 0.1 GB, giving 2.0 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 2.6 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Starter 4 GB with 4 GB.

Disk works the same way: 15 GB to begin with, plus 0.1 GB per user, so 15.5 GB against the 80 GB the plan includes.

Rust server with PostgreSQL and one or more workers. Faster per job than the Node-based options, which matters if the workflow count is high.
