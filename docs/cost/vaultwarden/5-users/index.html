Self-hosting Vaultwarden for 5 people: the cost

At 5 users, Vaultwarden needs 0.3 GB of memory and 2.1 GB of disk. That fits the Basic 512 MiB at DigitalOcean for $4.00 a month. Add backups and the time it takes to look after and the real figure is $42.30 a month.

How 0.3 GB was arrived at

0.2 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 5 users at 1 MB each adds 0 GB, giving 0.2 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 0.3 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Basic 512 MiB with 0.5 GB.

Disk works the same way: 2 GB to begin with, plus 0.02 GB per user, so 2.1 GB against the 10 GB the plan includes.

A Rust reimplementation of the Bitwarden server. It runs comfortably in a fraction of a gigabyte because vault data is small and encrypted client-side.

Where to put Vaultwarden

0.3 GB of memory and 2.1 GB of disk is what this needs at 5 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the Basic 512 MiB at DigitalOcean for $4.00 a month.

We are not claiming this is the cheapest server on the internet. It is the cheapest one we can honestly quote. Hetzner Cloud, Vultr and PikaPods publish prices we could not read programmatically, and Hetzner in particular is usually cheaper per gigabyte of memory than anything quoted above.
