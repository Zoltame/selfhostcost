How 2.7 GB was arrived at

2 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 10 users at 15 MB each adds 0.1 GB, giving 2.1 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 2.7 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Pro plus a 600 GB volume with 4 GB.

Disk works the same way: 20 GB to begin with, plus 60 GB per user, so 620 GB against the 20 GB the plan includes. The shortfall is bought as a 600 GB volume at $30.00 a month, which costs less than the next plan up. This tool is storage-hungry per user, so disk rather than memory is what will force your next upgrade.

Go with MariaDB and on-device classification. Lighter than Immich but its multi-user story is weaker.
