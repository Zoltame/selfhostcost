Self-hosting Gatus for 10 people: the cost

At 10 users, Gatus needs 0.3 GB of memory and 2.1 GB of disk. That fits the Basic 512 MiB at DigitalOcean for $4.00 a month. Add backups and the time it takes to look after and the real figure is $42.30 a month.

How 0.3 GB was arrived at

0.2 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 10 users at 0.5 MB each adds 0 GB, giving 0.2 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 0.3 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Basic 512 MiB with 0.5 GB.

Disk works the same way: 2 GB to begin with, plus 0.01 GB per user, so 2.1 GB against the 10 GB the plan includes.

Configuration as YAML in version control, which suits teams that want their monitoring reviewed like code. The smallest footprint on this entire site.
