Self-hosting Metabase for 5 people: the cost

At 5 users, Metabase needs 2.6 GB of memory and 10.2 GB of disk. That fits the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month. Add backups and the time it takes to look after and the real figure is $141 a month.

How 2.6 GB was arrived at

2 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 5 users at 15 MB each adds 0.1 GB, giving 2.1 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 2.6 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Basic 4 GiB with 4 GB.

Disk works the same way: 10 GB to begin with, plus 0.05 GB per user, so 10.2 GB against the 80 GB the plan includes.

JVM application with an application database of its own. Concurrent query load, not seat count, is what forces an upgrade here.
