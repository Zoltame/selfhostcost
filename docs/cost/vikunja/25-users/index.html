How 0.7 GB was arrived at

0.5 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 25 users at 3 MB each adds 0.1 GB, giving 0.6 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 0.7 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Basic with 1 GB.

Disk works the same way: 5 GB to begin with, plus 0.1 GB per user, so 7.5 GB against the 20 GB the plan includes.

A single Go binary with SQLite, PostgreSQL or MySQL. Kanban, list and Gantt views without the operational weight of Plane.
