Self-hosting Apache Superset for 5 people: the cost

At 5 users, Apache Superset needs 5.1 GB of memory and 20.2 GB of disk. That fits the Basic 8 GiB at DigitalOcean for $48.00 a month. Add backups and the time it takes to look after and the real figure is $283 a month.

How 5.1 GB was arrived at

4 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 5 users at 20 MB each adds 0.1 GB, giving 4.1 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 5.1 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Basic 8 GiB with 8 GB.

Disk works the same way: 20 GB to begin with, plus 0.05 GB per user, so 20.2 GB against the 160 GB the plan includes.

Web process, Celery workers, beat scheduler, PostgreSQL and Redis. More capable than Metabase and considerably more to operate.

Where to put Apache Superset

5.1 GB of memory and 20.2 GB of disk is what this needs at 5 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the Basic 8 GiB at DigitalOcean for $48.00 a month.
