Self-hosting Keycloak for 100 people: the cost

At 100 users, Keycloak needs 2.7 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk. That fits the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month. Add backups and the time it takes to look after and the real figure is $141 a month.

How 2.7 GB was arrived at

2 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 100 users at 2 MB each adds 0.2 GB, giving 2.2 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 2.7 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Basic 4 GiB with 4 GB.

Disk works the same way: 10 GB to begin with, plus 0.01 GB per user, so 11 GB against the 80 GB the plan includes.

JVM service with PostgreSQL. Memory is dominated by the heap rather than by user count, so cost per user falls steeply as the directory grows. This is the CNCF-backed incumbent.

Where to put Keycloak

2.7 GB of memory and 11 GB of disk is what this needs at 100 people. The cheapest plan that fits, among the providers whose prices we have read ourselves, is the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month.
