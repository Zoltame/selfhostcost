Self-hosting Zitadel for 25 people: the cost

At 25 users, Zitadel needs 2.6 GB of memory and 10.2 GB of disk. That fits the Basic 4 GiB at DigitalOcean for $24.00 a month. Add backups and the time it takes to look after and the real figure is $141 a month.

How 2.6 GB was arrived at

2 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 25 users at 2 MB each adds 0.1 GB, giving 2.0 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 2.6 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Basic 4 GiB with 4 GB.

Disk works the same way: 10 GB to begin with, plus 0.01 GB per user, so 10.2 GB against the 80 GB the plan includes.

Go binary over PostgreSQL, with an event-sourced audit log. Lighter than the JVM option and closer to Auth0's developer ergonomics.
