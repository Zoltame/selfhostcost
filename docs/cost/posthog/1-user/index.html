Self-hosting PostHog for 1 person: the cost

At 1 user, PostHog needs 10.0 GB of memory and 60.2 GB of disk. That fits the Basic 16 GiB at DigitalOcean for $96.00 a month. Add backups and the time it takes to look after and the real figure is $340 a month.

How 10.0 GB was arrived at

8 GB covers the stack at rest, every container the project ships included. 1 user at 15 MB each adds 0.0 GB, giving 8.0 GB. Adding 25% headroom so the machine is not run at its ceiling gives 10.0 GB, and the smallest plan at or above that is the Basic 16 GiB with 16 GB.

Disk works the same way: 60 GB to begin with, plus 0.2 GB per user, so 60.2 GB against the 320 GB the plan includes.

Kafka, ClickHouse, PostgreSQL, Redis and several workers. PostHog itself now discourages self-hosting below a substantial scale, and the sizing here reflects that honestly.
