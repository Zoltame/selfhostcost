How this works

A paid seat has no floor and a steep slope: ten people cost ten times one person. A server has a high floor and almost no slope: the same machine serves ten people or a hundred. Every comparison on this site is really just the point where those two lines cross, and that point moves depending on how heavy the tool is and what your time is worth.

We size each tool from its own documented requirements where they exist, pick the smallest server plan that actually fits, add backups, and add the hours. Prices carry the date they were read and a link to the page they were read from. When we have not checked a price, we do not publish a figure that depends on it. The full method is here, including everything the model deliberately ignores.
